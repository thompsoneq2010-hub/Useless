THE ORIGINAL SIX

In 1975, six teenage boys entered the Raven Creek area for essentially the same reason the 1990 group would return fifteen years later: they wanted to investigate the stories surrounding the abandoned town, cemetery and asylum.

Five died. One survived.

The survivor's account became the foundation of the legend, but it never provided a complete explanation. Were the boys attacked by something supernatural? Did fear turn them against each other? Was the cult responsible? Or was the survivor hiding something?
