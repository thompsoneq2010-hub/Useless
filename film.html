THE PROJECT

THE RAVEN
CREEK TAPES

A period-found-footage horror film about six teenagers, one unsolved case and a place where fear can become its own evidence.

THE FILM

1990

The main film takes place in 1990. The group documents the investigation with period-appropriate cameras, tapes, film photography and audio equipment.

No smartphones. No GPS. No internet. No instant answers.

The camera is part of the story. What it sees is limited by where the operator points it, when the camera is running and what the group chooses to document.

THE IDEA

THE MONSTER DOESN'T HAVE TO BE THE ONLY THING PEOPLE ARE AFRAID OF.

The story is built around paranoia, uncertainty and the breakdown of trust. The audience should never receive an easy answer about whether the supernatural is real.

PRODUCTION

COMING TO LIFE

Production is planned to begin in 2027. The project is currently developing its story, gathering period equipment, building the archive and raising funds.
