A PLACE PEOPLE STOPPED TALKING ABOUT

Raven Creek was once a small community surrounded by dense woods. The town eventually emptied, leaving behind buildings, graves and the abandoned asylum that became central to local stories.

Over time, rumors connected the area to a cult. Accounts differ on what the group believed, what happened at the asylum, and how much of the legend is based on fact.

One thing is consistent: people who investigate Raven Creek tend to leave with more questions than answers.
