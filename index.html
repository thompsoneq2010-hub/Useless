THE CASE

SOMETHING HAPPENED AT RAVEN CREEK.

In 1975, six teenagers entered the Raven Creek area. Five died. One survived. Fifteen years later, another six returned to document the truth.

Their route led through the woods, a graveyard, an abandoned town, back into the woods, and finally toward the old asylum.
